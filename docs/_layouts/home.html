Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free and open source? expand_more
Yes, Watch Later Button is 100% free and open-source under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0). There are no paid tiers, no ads, and no hidden subscriptions.
How do I customize the button color and transparency? expand_more
Click the Watch Later Button icon in your browser toolbar to open the popup, then select Settings. You can choose any HEX or RGB color, adjust the opacity slider from 0% to 100%, and preview changes immediately on a checkerboard stage. All open YouTube tabs update live in real-time.
Can I undo if I click the button by mistake? expand_more
Yes! Whenever you save a video, an undo prompt appears for 10 seconds. Clicking it immediately removes the video from your Watch Later playlist.
Does the extension collect or transmit my data? expand_more
No. The extension does not communicate with external servers and collects zero personal data. All operations happen directly between your browser and YouTube's existing client interface, and color settings are stored only in your browser's local storage.
Which browsers and platforms are supported? expand_more
Watch Later Button supports Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
Does it work on YouTube Shorts and video cards? expand_more
Yes! The button is automatically injected into standard video watch pages, YouTube Shorts player controls, and thumbnail cards across home, subscriptions, and search feeds.
