## Introduction

Welcome to the Watch Later Button extension for YouTube™. This extension is designed to provide a simple and efficient way to add videos to your "Watch Later" playlist on YouTube. Your privacy is important to me, and this policy is intended to help you understand what information I handle and your rights.

**Last Updated:** November 15, 2026

## Data Collection and Usage

The Watch Later Button extension **does not include analytics or send your viewing activity to me.**

Saving or undoing a video uses YouTube's existing client interface to send a playlist request to YouTube for your signed-in account. Your appearance and control preferences are stored in your browser and may be synchronized through your browser account, as described below. No extension activity or preferences are sent to my servers.

## Permissions

To function correctly, the Watch Later Button extension requests the following permissions:

*   **Host permission (`https://www.youtube.com/*`):** Used exclusively to:
    *   Identify the video currently being viewed.
    *   Add the "Save to Watch Later" button below the video player, in the Shorts action column, and on video cards.
    *   Dispatch the "add" or "undo" action to YouTube's client interface when you interact with the button.
*   **Storage permission (`storage`):** Stores your button color, opacity, keyboard shortcut, undo duration, surface controls, and update-notification preference in `storage.local` and `storage.sync`. If browser-account synchronization is enabled, your browser may transmit these preferences through its sync service to your other devices. The extension does not include video IDs, watch history, or account credentials in these settings, and I do not receive them.

I do not use any permissions to track your browsing history or collect any personal information.

## Legal Basis (GDPR)

**For the Watch Later Button Extension:** I do not receive your playlist activity or extension preferences. YouTube processes playlist requests, and your browser provider may process synchronized preferences under their respective privacy policies.

**For the Website:** This website collects analytics data through Umami. The processing of analytics data is based on my legitimate interest under GDPR Article 6(1)(f) in understanding how users interact with my website and improving the user experience. This constitutes a balance between my legitimate interests and your privacy expectations.

## Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR and German data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding data collected on my website:

*   **Right of access:** You may request information about what analytics data I have about your visits
*   **Right to deletion:** You can opt out of analytics collection (see [Your Rights and Opt-Out](#your-rights-and-opt-out))
*   **Right to data portability:** I can provide a summary of the data collected about you
*   **Right to object:** You can object to analytics data collection at any time
*   **Right to withdraw consent:** You can opt out of analytics tracking using Do Not Track (DNT)

For the **Watch Later Button Extension:** I do not hold a record of your playlist activity or extension preferences. You can manage your playlists on YouTube and your saved preferences through the extension and browser settings.

To exercise any of these rights or for any data protection inquiries, please contact me at the address provided in my [Legal Notice (Impressum)](/impressum/).

## Website Analytics and Cookies

This website uses **Umami Analytics**, a privacy-focused analytics tool that is **self-hosted on my own infrastructure** at `https://umami.alxple.com/`. Because Umami runs on my own servers, all analytics data remains under my complete control and is never shared with any third-party services. Umami is designed with privacy in mind and fully complies with GDPR requirements.

### Data Collected by Umami

When you visit my website, Umami collects the following data:

- **Visited pages and sections** - Which pages you view
- **Browser information** - Browser type, operating system, screen size
- **Referrer information** - Where you came from (if applicable)
- **Geographic location** - Country level (inferred from anonymized IP data)
- **Device type** - Desktop, tablet, or mobile
- **User interactions** - Button clicks, form submissions

**Important:** All data is stored exclusively on my own servers and never transmitted to any external services.

Umami **does not**:
- Use cookies or persistent identifiers
- Track individual users across websites
- Share data with any third parties (data never leaves my servers)
- Store IP addresses in full (they are hashed and discarded)
- Engage in retargeting or advertising

### My Use of Analytics Data

I use this analytics data solely to:
- Understand which features and pages are most useful to visitors
- Identify technical issues or problems
- Improve the overall user experience
- Monitor website performance and uptime

### Legal Basis

The processing of your data through Umami is based on my legitimate interest in understanding website usage and improving my service (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

### Your Rights and Opt-Out

You can prevent Umami from collecting your data by:

- **Using Do Not Track (DNT):** If your browser is configured to send a "Do Not Track" signal, Umami respects this preference
- **Browser extensions:** Use privacy-focused browser extensions to block analytics scripts
- **Contacting me:** Email me at the address in my [Impressum](/impressum/) if you have concerns about data collection

### Data Retention

Umami retains analytics data for **90 days** by default. After this period, the data is automatically deleted.

## Third-Party Services

*   **YouTube:** When you use the extension with YouTube, your interaction is governed by YouTube's Privacy Policy.
*   **Browser Store:** Download pages and reviews are hosted by Google Chrome Web Store and Mozilla Firefox Add-ons, which have their own privacy policies.

**Note:** Website analytics (Umami) are **not** processed by third parties. All analytics data is collected and stored exclusively on my own servers.
