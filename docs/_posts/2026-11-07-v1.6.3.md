## Custom keyboard shortcut: Save videos without touching the mouse

Version 1.6.3 introduces one of the most requested features from power users: **fully configurable keyboard shortcuts**. You can now save or remove videos from your Watch Later playlist in a fraction of a second using a simple keypress.

- **Universal hotkey (`Alt + W` by default)**: Works seamlessly across all YouTube surfaces:
  - **Watch Pages**: Save the currently playing video immediately.
  - **YouTube Shorts**: Save the active Short without breaking your viewing flow.
  - **Feed & Thumbnail Cards**: Hover over any video thumbnail on your Home, Subscriptions, or Search feeds and press `Alt + W` to save it directly.
- **In-popup shortcut recorder**: Open the extension popup, go to **Settings &rarr; Controls & Timing**, and click the shortcut badge to record your preferred key combination (e.g. `Ctrl + Shift + S`, `Alt + S`, or any custom combo) or press `Esc` to cancel.
- **Smart input guard**: The shortcut handler automatically disables itself whenever your focus is inside a search input, comment box, or chat window, preventing accidental playlist triggers while typing.

## Configurable undo timer: Tailored to your pace

We know everyone navigates YouTube differently. While some users love the safety net of the 10-second undo prompt, others prefer instant saves with zero delay, or a longer window to change their mind.

Version 1.6.3 gives you total control over the undo window duration:

- **Preset durations**: Choose between **Off** (instant save), **10s** (default), or **15s** with a single click.
- **Custom duration stepper**: Need something specific? The custom stepper allows you to set any duration from **1 to 60 seconds**.
- **Instant mode (Off)**: When turned off, clicking the button instantly saves the video and flashes a confirmation badge without opening a countdown ring or undo prompt.
- **Dynamic circular countdown**: The visual `conic-gradient` timer ring around the player button automatically adjusts its rotation speed to match your selected duration.

## YouTube Shorts & feed observer resilience

YouTube frequently tests dynamic DOM updates and single-page container swaps. Version 1.6.3 adds hardened safeguards to ensure uninterrupted reliability:

- **Clean Shorts transitions**: When scrolling between Shorts videos, the previous video's undo state is automatically cleared, ensuring the button on the newly active Short always reflects its accurate state.
- **Duplicate button prevention**: Enhanced selector scoping guarantees that only a single Watch Later button is injected per thumbnail card, even on heavy infinite-scroll feeds.
- **Context-aware mutation observer**: Optimized DOM observation minimizes CPU overhead and eliminates layout thrashing during rapid page scrolling.

## Settings redesign & update notification control

- **Grouped settings sections**: The popup settings are now neatly categorized into **Appearance** (color, transparency, checkerboard preview), **Controls & Timing** (hotkeys, undo duration), and **Notifications**.
- **Update notifications toggle**: You can now choose whether to automatically open the "What's new" release notes tab after extension updates.
- **Live website playground**: Our official website ([watchlater.alxple.com](https://watchlater.alxple.com/)) has also been upgraded with an interactive studio simulating both the YouTube player and the authentic extension settings popup in real time!
