## Milestone Release: Watch Later Button v1.6.5

Version 1.6.5 is a major architectural and functional evolution for the extension. I've redesigned the entire extension pipeline from the ground up into a single, unified source of truth (`src/`), added granular controls over where buttons appear across YouTube, brought dynamic keyboard shortcut hints directly to button tooltips, and extended the typography engine with native Cyrillic and Latin-Extended font support.

---

## 1. Granular Surface Controls: Put Buttons Exactly Where You Want

Previously, Watch Later buttons were automatically mounted across all YouTube surfaces: the main video player, YouTube Shorts, and thumbnail cards throughout your feeds.

In Version 1.6.5, I'm putting full control in your hands:

- **Dedicated Surfaces Settings**: Open the extension popup, head to **Settings &rarr; Surfaces**, and independently toggle buttons for:
  - **Video Player (`/watch`)**: The primary button beneath the video player next to YouTube's native action buttons.
  - **YouTube Shorts (`/shorts`)**: The button within the right-side vertical action column on Shorts reels.
  - **Video Cards**: Hover/mount buttons on video thumbnails across Home, Subscriptions, Channel pages, and Search results.
- **Reactive Instant Updates**: Changes apply in real time via `chrome.storage.onChanged` across all currently open YouTube tabs. Turning off a surface immediately detaches its buttons and cleans up background observers without requiring a page reload.
- **Smart Shortcut Scoping**: When a surface is toggled off, pressing the global shortcut (<kbd>Alt + W</kbd>) while browsing that surface will respectfully do nothing.

---

## 2. Dynamic Hotkey Hints in Button Tooltips

Power users who configured custom shortcuts in version 1.6.3 will love this enhancement:

- **Context-Aware Tooltips**: The button's tooltip (`title` and `aria-label`) now dynamically reflects your configured hotkey. For example:
  - Default: `Watch Later (Alt + W)` (or localized equivalent, e.g. `Смотреть позже (Alt + W)`).
  - Custom combination: e.g. `Watch Later (Ctrl + Shift + S)`.
  - Disabled / No shortcut: Clean fallback to `Watch Later`.
- **Undo State Preservation**: During an active undo countdown, the tooltip dynamically switches to `Delete from Watch Later` (or localized counterpart) and restores the hotkey hint once the undo window completes.

---

## 3. Extended Multi-Language Typography (`Cyrillic` & `Latin-Ext`)

Watch Later Button natively supports 26 languages across the globe. To guarantee pristine, high-density typographic rendering in the popup and onboarding pages for all users:

- **Complete Font Subsets**: Bundled official Google Fonts WOFF2 subsets for **Inter** (regular 400 & medium 500) and **Manrope** (bold 700) covering:
  - **Cyrillic & Cyrillic-Ext** (Ukrainian, Russian, Bulgarian, etc.)
  - **Latin-Extended** (Czech, Polish, Turkish, Romanian, German, Swedish, Norwegian, etc.)
  - **Latin** (English, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, etc.)
- **Zero Fallback Glitches**: No more font mismatches or fallback to generic system fonts on accented letters, umlauts, or non-Latin glyphs.

---

## 4. Unified Architecture & Automated Release Pipeline

Behind the scenes, Version 1.6.5 transitions the repository away from duplicate browser directories to a streamlined, modern architecture:

- **Unified `src/` Codebase**: All content scripts, background workers, locales, assets, and UI panels are maintained in one single folder.
- **Zero-Dependency Fast Builder**: A native Node.js builder (`scripts/build.mjs`) automatically compiles target-optimized builds for Chrome (MV3 Service Worker) and Firefox (MV3 Background Script + AMO compatibility checks), enforcing zero link leaks.
- **Automated Validation**: Integrated Puppeteer test suites ensure that all 1,560+ localized elements across all 26 languages and popup dimensions remain pixel-perfect and error-free on every release.
